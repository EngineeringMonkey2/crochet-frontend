Application Process

Getting started is simple. Our application process is designed to ensure we can provide the best possible support for your business while maintaining the quality standards our customers expect.

Upon approval, our dedicated team will work closely with you to:

  • Set up your professional Stripe merchant account
  • Configure your custom storefront and branding
  • Import your product catalog and optimize listings
  • Train you on our dashboard and analytics tools
  • Establish your shipping and fulfillment preferences
  • Feature your products on our platform with promotional support

Company Showcase: Approved partners will have their company name, logo, and location featured prominently on our website, giving you additional exposure and building customer trust through transparency.

Ongoing Support: Our relationship doesn't end at launch. We provide continuous support, regular platform updates, marketing opportunities, and business growth consultations to ensure your long-term success.
